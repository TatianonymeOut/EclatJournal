# Medical disclaimer

Éclat Journal is a personal journaling and record-keeping tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, clinical monitoring, medication management, or emergency services.

## Use health notes carefully

You may use the app to write down symptoms, medication names, doses, dates, and personal observations. The information you enter is only a personal record. It may be incomplete, inaccurate, unavailable, or unsuitable for clinical use.

Do not make, delay, stop, or change a medical decision, prescription, dose, or treatment plan based on information displayed by Éclat Journal. Verify medication information with a qualified healthcare professional or pharmacist.

Trends, calendars, mood summaries, and other views are descriptive only. They do not identify causes, predict outcomes, detect emergencies, or replace professional assessment.

## When to seek help

If you think you may have a medical emergency, seek immediate help from local emergency services or an appropriate emergency healthcare provider. If you have concerns about symptoms, medication, mental health, or wellbeing, contact a qualified healthcare professional.

Éclat Journal does not monitor entries in real time, alert a clinician, or contact anyone on your behalf.

## Privacy reminder

Health notes can be sensitive. Consider the security of your device, backups, shared access, and any export destination before entering or sharing them. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) for the app's intended data-handling approach.
